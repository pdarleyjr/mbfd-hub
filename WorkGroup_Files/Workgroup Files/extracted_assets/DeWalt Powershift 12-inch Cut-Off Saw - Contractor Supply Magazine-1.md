## DeWalt Powershift 12-inch Cut-Off Saw

DeWalt POWERSHIFT 12-inch Cut-Off Saw manages cuts up to 4 3/4 inches deep through concrete, rebar, ductile iron and more. Utilizing a gear-driven design and the POWERSHIFT battery, users can slice up to 8 linear feet of concrete or make up to 156 cuts in #5 rebar on a single charge. Integrated base wheels enable quick adjustments and optimal cutting angles. For user safety, the saw features an electric brake that stops the blade in as little as three seconds after the trigger is released. Slated to be available in fall 2026 kitted with two POWERSHIFT batteries and a charger.
