## HURST CUTTING TECHNOLOGY

With intuitive blade designs engineered to pull material to the center of the blades, industry-leading cutting force at the point of attack and leading ergonomic design, HURST Jaws of Life ®  cutters are undeniably the industry standard.

Years ago, industry challengers began hyping 'maximum cutting force' as the key selling point when considering cutters. This created an inaccurate standard of - the more force, the better.

This focus on 'maximum cutting force' has created confusion in the marketplace regarding how cutting forces are calculated, why advertised cutting forces may vary from National Fire Protection Agency (NFPA) guidelines and how some manufacturers inflate cutting-force measurements for marketing purposes.
