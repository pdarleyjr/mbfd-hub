# MBFD Support Services Guidance

Short, member-safe guidance for answering low-risk "how do I" and "where do I" questions about Support Services, vehicle checkouts and station requests in the Hub. Anything involving safety, readiness, protective gear, medical items, radios or lighting, or anything expensive, unusual or unclear, stays with staff and gets a plain acknowledgment.

## Acknowledgment and Tone
<!-- kb slug="acknowledgment-and-tone" tags="tone,acknowledgment,reply,receipt,style" review="2027-05-01" -->
Replies are brief. Most are just "Acknowledged." A low-risk how-do-I or where-do-I question gets one to three plain sentences, using only this guidance.

"Acknowledged." means the Hub received the message. It does not mean a staff member has accepted the problem, ordered anything or scheduled a repair. If a member asks, say so plainly. If it matters and nothing has happened, the Company Officer can follow up with Support Services leadership directly, and through 300 if response ability is affected.

Never promise stock, a spare, a pickup time, a turnaround, a repair or an approval. Never give names, phone numbers or room locations, and never quote or name a policy or document. A request that is incomplete or unanswered is not approved.

If unsure, or the topic is safety, readiness, protective gear, medical, radios, lighting or something expensive, reply "Acknowledged." and leave it to staff.

## Missing Low-Value Consumables
<!-- kb slug="missing-low-value-consumables" tags="missing,consumable,supplies,kitty-litter,absorbent,tape,batteries,checkout" review="2027-05-01" -->
Low-cost supplies such as kitty litter, absorbent, paper towels, trash bags, zip ties, tape, cleaning supplies and AA or AAA batteries are noncritical.

- First check the station for extra stock, including other compartments and other units at the station.
- If it is still missing, call or text before coming by Station 2 so staff can check on a replacement. Do not show up unannounced.
- Staff cannot promise stock or a date.
- Mark the item Missing in the Vehicle Checkout so the record is accurate.

If the missing item stops the unit from doing its job, it is not a routine supply question. Tell the Company Officer.

## Missing DeWalt Batteries and Shared Tools
<!-- kb slug="missing-dewalt-batteries-and-shared-tools" tags="dewalt,battery,blower,tools,missing,interchangeable,spanner,station-2" review="2027-05-01" -->
DeWalt tool batteries fit more than one tool, including the station blower. A "missing" battery is often in another tool, on a charger or in another compartment.

- Check the blower, other DeWalt tools, chargers and compartments before reporting it missing.
- Other removable tool batteries, such as M18 packs, get the same check across other tools and the apparatus.
- If it is still missing, call or text before coming by Station 2 so staff can check on a replacement.
- Use only the battery and charger made for each tool. Never force or mix them. A battery that is hot, swollen or leaking: stop using it, set it aside and tell the officer.

Routine items such as spanners, hydrant wrenches, door wedges, brooms, squeegees, shovels, cones and rubber mallets also move between compartments and units. Check there first.

A missing or damaged power tool itself, a saw, or any rescue tool is not routine. Tell the Company Officer.

## Expensive, Critical or Damaged Equipment
<!-- kb slug="equipment-expensive-critical-or-damaged" tags="expensive,critical,damaged,tag,officer,replacement,readiness" review="2027-05-01" -->
Some items never get routine "check for extra" advice: breathing apparatus, radios, lighting, rescue and cutting tools, hose and nozzles, protective gear, medical items, keys, tablets, laptops, generators, fans and chargers. The same goes for anything damaged, unusual or unclear.

- Tell the Company Officer right away, by voice. A Hub entry does not replace that.
- The officer tells 300 when the unit's ability to respond is affected or uncertain.
- Stop using damaged or defective equipment, tag it and keep it off the apparatus. Do not repair it or keep using it.
- Record it in the Vehicle Checkout so staff can see it.

Replacement of anything that is not routine goes through the officer's paperwork and staff review. Replacing safety equipment is not held up for missing paperwork. The assistant only acknowledges these reports. It does not say where a replacement will come from or when.

## Repeat Issues and the Next Checkout
<!-- kb slug="repeat-issue-and-next-checkout" tags="repeat,recurring,open-issue,checkout,history,status,inventory-correction" review="2027-05-01" -->
The Vehicle Checkout shows how many issues are already open on that apparatus. Each one sits beside its item with its status, who first reported it and a timeline of what has happened.

- Still record what you see today. If an item should be there and is not, mark it Missing even if it was reported before.
- Do not mark something OK because someone already reported it, and do not mark a check you could not do as passed.
- A submitted report is not a repair. Read the status on the issue card before assuming it is fixed.
- If the checklist itself is wrong (item moved, replaced, or not listed), use Report inventory correction or Report an unlisted item in that compartment.
- If the same problem keeps coming back or the status is unclear, tell the Company Officer or send a Support Services message.

Only do the checks the checkout asks for. If something looks unsafe, stop and tell the officer rather than testing or fixing it.

## Station Requests and Facilities
<!-- kb slug="station-request-and-facilities" tags="station-request,facilities,repair,hazard,exhaust,supplies,inventory-form" review="2027-05-01" -->
Use a Station Request for a station repair, service visit, room asset or equipment. From Home open Station / Vehicles / Equipment, pick the station, then Station Request. It is also in the Forms Hub.

- Choose Repair / Service or Equipment, the room (or station-wide), a priority, a clear description, and a photo if it helps.
- Tick "unusable / out of service" if the item or area cannot be used.
- Status updates and replies show in the station's Requests tab.
- For station supplies, use the Station Inventory Form.
- Air conditioner filters that need replacing go in a Station Request.

A hazard comes first. Electrical problems, gas smells, water intrusion, structural damage, a missing pole-hole cover or a failed exhaust-recovery system: tell the Station Officer now and keep people out of the area. The form does not replace that, and a Critical priority is not a phone call. Never attempt an electrical or restricted repair yourself.

## Bunker Gear Drop-Off
<!-- kb slug="bunker-gear-drop-off" tags="bunker-gear,turnout,cleaning,contamination,message-support-services,replacement" review="2027-05-01" -->
Gear used in fire conditions, heavily soiled, exposed to chemicals, pesticides or bloodborne pathogens, needing repair, or that you are simply worried about, should go in for cleaning. Do not wait for visible damage. Officers inspect gear on Mondays.

- Do not wash it in station machines or take it home.
- Do not put unassessed contaminated gear with clean supplies or in living areas.
- Send a Bunker gear cleaning request from Message Support Services on the Home screen. Say why it needs cleaning, when it was left, your next duty date, any repairs needed, and whether you need replacement gear.
- Staff confirm where to bring it. Leaving gear somewhere is not a service request by itself.
- Do not assume a turnaround time. Staff will tell you.

Unknown chemicals or an exposure: tell the officer first. Exposure reporting and medical follow-up do not wait for the gear. If you need replacement gear, tell the officer. After hours the officer goes through 300. The assistant only replies "Acknowledged."

## How the Hub Replaces Old Paper and Email Steps
<!-- kb slug="hub-digital-workflow-crosswalk" tags="hub,workflow,logbook,email,checkout,messages,station-request,service-request" review="2027-05-01" -->
- Apparatus logbook and daily inspection sheet: the Vehicle Checkout. Findings reach staff from it. No second handwritten log or duplicate email is needed for the same item.
- Emailing Support Services about a repair: record it in the checkout. For a mechanical problem outside a checkout, use Apparatus Service in the Employee Portal.
- Station repair or equipment email: Station Request. Supplies: Station Inventory Form.
- Emailing about bunker gear: Message Support Services, reason Bunker gear cleaning request.
- Questions and equipment requests: Message Support Services. Replies appear in My Messages, with a notification.
- Checklist wrong or an item not listed: Report inventory correction or Report an unlisted item in the checkout.

Calling the Company Officer, 300 or the on-duty contact for urgent matters still happens. A Hub entry does not replace it, and priority settings do not change a unit's status. If the Hub is unavailable, tell the officer, use the paper backup and enter it when the Hub returns.

## Station 2 and Weekend Handoff
<!-- kb slug="station2-and-weekend-handoff" tags="station-2,weekend,saturday,sunday,spare,300,replacement,after-hours" review="2027-05-01" -->
For routine missing items, staff may check on a replacement through Station 2. Call or text first. Do not come by without contacting staff.

On Saturday and Sunday, if a noncritical item is needed right away, contact 300 to check whether a spare is available. That is a check, not a promise that a spare exists. It applies only to noncritical needs, not to safety, readiness or protective equipment.

After hours, only 300 or the ranking officer on the unit starts a call for apparatus service. Never call an off-duty technician. An unanswered call or a closed shop does not lift a restriction on an unsafe unit, and 300 decides coverage.

## Who Handles What
<!-- kb slug="contact-and-routing-roles" tags="roles,routing,officer,300,support-services,fleet,air-tech,who" review="2027-05-01" -->
Roles only. Find current contacts in the department directory.

- Company Officer: first stop for any problem with a unit, equipment or crew. Tells 300 when response is affected.
- Station Officer: station hazards and station requests.
- 300 (Suppression Division Chief): coverage, unit status and after-hours decisions.
- Support Services, also called Logistics: supplies, equipment service and replacement, bunker gear cleaning, loans, facilities coordination. The Logistics Captain and the Logistics Division Chief lead it.
- Fleet: whether a vehicle can move and apparatus repairs. In normal hours the Fleet Operations Manager is the first call, then the Logistics Captain, then the Logistics Division Chief. Shop technicians are a last resort.
- Air Tech: breathing apparatus and gas monitors.
- EMS: medical supplies and equipment, not Support Services.
- Public Safety Communications: radio programming and communications changes.

## When to Escalate
<!-- kb slug="when-to-escalate" tags="escalate,safety,readiness,contamination,hazard,unknown,expensive,conflict,urgent" review="2027-05-01" -->
Answer only "Acknowledged." and leave it to staff when a message involves any of these:

- Safety or readiness: breathing apparatus, protective gear, radios, lighting, pumps, aerial devices, brakes, steering, tires, or anything that might keep a unit from responding.
- Contamination or exposure, or an injury.
- Station hazards: electrical, gas, smoke, water intrusion, structural damage, failed exhaust recovery.
- Anything expensive, unique or damaged, or an item the guidance does not clearly cover.
- Facts that conflict, or a member who is unsure.

Urgent matters need a verbal report to the Company Officer now. The officer notifies 300 when response is affected. A Hub message does not replace that, and it never replaces a required Risk Management, police or legal report. A vehicle accident, property damage or theft has its own separate reports.

## Borrowing Equipment for Public Education
<!-- kb slug="equipment-loans-public-education" tags="loan,borrow,public-education,demonstration,equipment,approval" review="2027-05-01" -->
Equipment is lent only for fire safety and public education demonstrations by a firefighter, never for personal use.

- Ask ahead of the event. Send a Support Services message with reason Equipment, and let your officer know. Include where and when, the audience and purpose, an itemized equipment list with how it will be used, and who is borrowing.
- No answer is not approval. Wait for a yes.
- Power tools (hydraulic, pneumatic or fuel), ladders and apparatus are never lent.
- Approved items are normally issued on your shift before the event and returned clean on your following shift. A weekend or relief-day exception must be recorded.
- A return is complete only when staff inspect it. Report delays, loss or damage to Support Services promptly.
