# Support Services Automation: a simple guide

You do not need to know anything technical. Everything is on the left side of the Admin screen under **Support Services Automation**.

## Turn it off (or on)

1. Click **Overview**.
2. At the top is a big box that says **AI AUTOMATIONS ON** or **OFF**.
3. Click **Turn OFF**. That's it. Nothing automatic is sent after that. Everything else in the Hub keeps working.
4. To turn it back on, click **Turn ON**, then **Turn ON** again in the box that pops up.

## Change how important an item is

1. Click **Equipment Priorities**.
2. Type the item in the search box (for example "helmet" or "kitty litter").
3. Click **Low**, **Medium** or **High** on that row. It saves right away.
   - Low: cheap, everyday items.
   - Medium: expensive or specialized items (power tools, gas meters, Lifepaks, SCBA cylinders).
   - High: firefighting gear (SCBA, mask, hood, helmet, bunker coat and pants) and anything unsafe.
4. Items no one has sorted yet are under **Not classified yet** (button at the top). Click Low, Medium or High next to each.
5. Made a mistake? Open the item and click **Restore previous**.
6. After changing priorities, click **Update open issues** to update reports that are already open. It shows you what will change first, and nobody gets notified.

## Change what an item's reply says

1. In **Equipment Priorities**, click the item.
2. Type the reply you want in **What should the reply say?** Leave it empty to use the normal reply.
3. The box on the right shows exactly what the member will see. Click **Save**.

## Change the standard replies

1. Click **Response Messages**.
2. Find the message (they are grouped: Missing items, Batteries, Air Tech, Captain 5, Station 2, Weekend, Safety).
3. Click **Edit**, change the words, check the preview, click **Save**. **Restore previous** undoes it.

## Change the instructions the AI uses for questions

1. Click **Knowledge & Instructions**.
2. Pick a topic (or **Add**), change the words, click **Preview**, then **Publish**.
3. You can also bring in a Word, PDF or text file with **Import**.
4. **Test a message** shows which instructions would be used for a sample question.

## See what was sent

Click **Activity & History**. Each line shows what came in, its priority, the exact reply, and whether staff still need to act (they always do: a reply only means "received").

## Pick which alerts you get

Click your name > **Notification Settings**, then under **Support Services alerts: priority levels** tick Low, Medium and/or High. Approvals and safety holds always reach you.

## Try something out without sending anything

On **Overview**, use **Try a message**: type an item or a message and see the priority and the reply. Nothing is saved or sent.
